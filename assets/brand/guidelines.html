01 · The mark

A stencil E with a fiducial.

The primary mark is a stencil capital “E” with a fiducial dot, in gold ENIG copper. The E is the wordmark initial; the stencil bridge-cuts and the fiducial speak the language of PCB fabrication — what etchy operates on.

stencil E (8 bars · 5 bridge gaps) + fiducial (pad in a clearance ring).

02 · Construction

Drawn on a 120-unit grid.

06 · The accent dot
FIDUCIAL — default
filled pad + clearance ring
VIA — alternate
hollow ring only

Use the fiducial as the default accent. The via (hollow ring) is a permitted alternate. Never use both, never enlarge the dot beyond the ring shown.

07 · Misuse
✗ Don’t recolor outside the palette
✗ Don’t rotate, skew or stretch
✗ Don’t add shadows, glows or bevels
✗ Don’t close the stencil bridge gaps
✗ Don’t crowd the clear space
✗ Don’t place on low-contrast backgrounds
